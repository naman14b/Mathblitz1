MathBlitz Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for the MathBlitz mobile application

Last Updated: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MathBlitz ("MathBlitz", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the MathBlitz mobile application.

MathBlitz is a math-learning and puzzle game that includes mental-math challenges, daily challenges, journey/progression features, Sudoku, math puzzles, leaderboards, optional AI coaching, and advertising features.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide in the app

  • Player name: If you choose a player name, it may be stored locally and may be sent to our server for features such as leaderboards, AI coaching, and Math Boss results.
  • Age group: You select an age group during onboarding. The selected age group is used to tailor math difficulty and advertising treatment. It may also be sent with leaderboard submissions.
  • Game progress: Scores, streaks, tokens, unlocked content, achievements, puzzle progress, journey progress, and related gameplay state may be stored locally on your device.
  • Gameplay performance: For AI coaching and learning features, MathBlitz may record information such as questions attempted, answers, correctness, topic/subtopic, difficulty, response time, and learning progress.

Leaderboard information

If you use leaderboard features, your chosen player name, score, selected age group, game mode, and submission time may be sent to our server and displayed through the in-app leaderboard.

AI Coach information

When AI Coach features are used, MathBlitz may send a player identifier (currently derived from the local player name), player name, math questions, answers, correctness, topics, difficulty, response time, and related learning information to our backend. This information is used to identify weak areas, generate learning insights, and provide personalized math explanations and practice.

Technical and advertising information

MathBlitz uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). AdMob and related Google services may process technical information such as device information, IP address, advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from network information, ad interactions, and other information described in Google's applicable privacy documentation. MathBlitz uses Google's consent tools and configures advertising treatment based on the age group selected in the app.

MathBlitz requests internet/network access to communicate with its backend and advertising services. The Android app may also request access to images when an authorized administrative feature is used to select images for challenge questions.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide and operate MathBlitz gameplay and progression.
  • Save and restore player progress and settings.
  • Provide leaderboards and game results.
  • Analyze math performance and provide AI Coach learning insights.
  • Deliver and manage advertisements, including optional rewarded ads.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the app and backend services.
  • Process and verify future Google Play purchases when real-money products are enabled.

3. Local Storage

MathBlitz stores game and profile information on your device using local storage. This can include your player name, selected age group, scores, streaks, tokens, unlocked/completed content, achievements, settings, gameplay attempts, and cached AI Coach information.

Some sensitive authentication-related values, where applicable, are stored using the device's secure storage facilities.

You can remove locally stored MathBlitz data by using the app's available reset/delete options or by uninstalling the app, subject to how your device handles application data. Server-side information may require a separate deletion request as described below.

4. Third-Party Services

Google AdMob

MathBlitz uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertising, including interstitial and rewarded advertisements. AdMob may process information as described in Google's privacy documentation. MathBlitz also uses Google's consent mechanism where applicable.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

OpenRouter / AI model providers

MathBlitz's AI Coach can use OpenRouter to access an AI model. Relevant coaching prompts and gameplay context may be transmitted through the backend to generate coaching responses. The information sent is limited to what is necessary for the requested AI coaching function.

For information about OpenRouter's handling of data, please review OpenRouter's Privacy Policy.

Google Play

The current release contains infrastructure prepared for Google Play Billing, but real-money Google Play products are not active in this release. If purchases are enabled in a future version, purchase information such as product identifiers and Google Play purchase tokens may be processed to verify transactions and provide purchased entitlements.

5. Children's Privacy

MathBlitz includes age groups for children and younger players. We take children's privacy seriously and configure advertising requests according to the selected age group, including child-directed treatment for younger age groups and appropriate under-age-of-consent settings.

We do not require a child to create an account with an email address, telephone number, or password to play the core game. Players should not enter sensitive personal information into a player name or other free-text field.

Parents or guardians who have questions about information associated with a child may contact us using the contact information below.

6. Data Retention

Local gameplay data remains on the device until it is deleted, reset, overwritten, or the application is uninstalled. Server-side records may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant features, maintain security, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and maintain legitimate business records.

AI Coach gameplay records and learning profiles may be retained to provide ongoing personalized learning features.

7. Data Deletion and Privacy Requests

You may request deletion of server-side information associated with your MathBlitz player name or player identifier by contacting us. Please include enough information for us to identify the relevant player record without sending unnecessary personal information.

We may need to retain limited information where required by law, for security, fraud prevention, transaction records, or other legitimate legal purposes.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information handled by MathBlitz. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when MathBlitz's features, services, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last Updated" date.
